# 🏔️ Welcome to Yam

**Yam** (Yet Another Markdown App) is a minimalist, theme-able editor designed for focus and aesthetics.

> "Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away."
> — *Antoine de Saint-Exupéry*

---

## 🎨 Features at a Glance

### 1. Typography & Themes
Yam isn't just about text; it's about *how* text feels.
- **Visual Theming**: Use the settings to tweak colors, spacing, and typography in real-time.
- **System Fonts**: Access your local typeface library.
- **Modes**: `Light`, `Dark`, and specific presets like *Dracula*, *Nord*, and *Solarized*.

### 2. Rich Content Support
We support the full **GitHub Flavored Markdown** spec.

#### Task Lists

## 🚀 Getting Started

1.  **Open Settings**: Click the gear icon at the bottom left.
2.  **Pick a Vibe**: Try the *Cyberpunk* or *Monokai* themes.
3.  **Set Defaults**: Configure your preferred view mode for startup vs. file opening in the **General** tab.

*Happy writing!*
